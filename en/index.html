Five reasons business social media “doesn’t work”

Small businesses know they need to run them — but every time it comes down to the same things. The result is the same: a random presence, not a systematic one.

01

No time

The owner is busy with operations, and social media is always the “last thing to do”. Month after month.

02

No skill

It is unclear what to write, about what, and in what tone. An empty editor is more intimidating than any amount of work.

03

No budget for help

An external team costs $800–3,000 a month — with returns you cannot control.

04

“Uninspiring” without a designer

Stock template images you would be embarrassed to publish. The brand is unrecognizable in the feed.

05

Inconsistency

They run it for two weeks and stop — the biggest killer of reach. Consistency matters more than talent.

Two modes

Control exactly where you want it

Start by approving every post — and trust the system with exactly as much as you are ready to.

Mode 1 · by default

Approval

The system keeps a queue of ready ideas and posts. You save the best, reject the rest — only what you approve is published.

  • Bulk-approve the week in one click
  • Chat edits: “make it shorter”, “add something about the weekend”
  • Reject with a reason — and the system will not suggest anything similar
  • Notifications with ready-to-go visuals: open — approve
Mode 2 · when ready

Autopilot

Content goes out without manual approval — within the rules you set yourself. On sensitive topics the system will always ask.

  • An “always ask” list: prices, promotions, serious promises
  • Pause for 7 or 30 days in one click
  • A weekly digest: what went out and how it performs
  • Moderation and fact-checking before every publication
