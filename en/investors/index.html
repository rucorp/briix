Summary

BRIIX — Your AI marketer, from your website URL. A SaaS platform that automates social media marketing for small businesses: enter your website URL — get a month of on-brand content, ready to publish. The category is the AI social media employee: not a tool, but an employee that works for $79/mo.

Product

One entry point — a URL. The output is regularly published on-brand content. The user spends about 10 minutes a week with the product — or none at all, on autopilot.

Brand DNA

The brand profile is extracted from the site: palette, fonts, tone, audience, USP, off-limits topics. A second model’s self-check verifies the facts against the site.

Content engine

50+ ideas across 8 rubrics, text and visual generation, per-channel adaptations, carousels, short video scripts, moderation and versioning.

Designer

A layered editor with a single document schema shared by the editor and the render service. Auto-layout for 1:1 / 4:5 / 9:16 / 1.91:1 formats.

Publishing

4 channels for the MVP, a calendar, approval mode and limited autopilot, idempotent delivery with retries, reliable account connections.

Analytics and feedback loop

Metrics feed back into generation: rubrics and formats that work gain weight. This turns the data into a compounding ML asset.

Moderation and safety

Fact-checking against the site, industry guardrails, “always ask” on sensitive topics. EU data, token encryption, GDPR.

Why this wins

The differentiation window is product, not technology. The depth of each layer (crawling and fact-checking, layered rendering, an idempotent scheduler, platform approvals, a growing feedback dataset) is not built over a weekend — that depth is what defends the position.

Three pillars: Brand DNA with visual identity extracted from the site · a layered visual library + Designer (content in the client's colors, not template images) · a feedback loop where every user action changes the next generation. The key proof is in the product — a before/after on the client's real site: URL → 6 posts in their style, before sign-up and before payment.

Plan and milestones

The MVP takes 6 weeks, followed by a closed beta and a public launch. In parallel, from week 2, the landing page goes through a smoke test on paid traffic ($500–1,000): positioning is validated before development finishes.

Week 0–2

Platform approvals (critical path), the AI gateway core, the crawler, Brand DNA with an eval on 30 sites, the preview flow.

Week 3–5

The visual engine and Designer, channel connections, publishing, payments, analytics, security. A feature-complete MVP.

Week 6–8+

Closed beta (20 users, 5 industries), public launch, phase 2: autopilot → inbound → ads → new channels.
